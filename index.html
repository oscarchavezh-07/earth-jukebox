Sound settings

Sets the order in which the regions of the image are played.
Higher values split the image into more, smaller regions, which gives more notes.

How to listen

  • Pitch. Cooler colors, such as blues, sound lower. Warmer colors, such as reds and yellows, and brighter areas sound higher.
  • Length and volume. Large, uniform regions give long, louder notes. Small regions with a lot of detail give short, softer notes.
  • Stereo position. Regions on the left of the image play in your left ear, and regions on the right play in your right ear.
