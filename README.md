# Hearth Documentation

The Hearth proyect converts NASA Scientific Visualization Studio imagery into an auditory experience, generating soundscapes through sonification.

## How to use

The site will display a picture taken from the NASA SVS alongside its description, encased in semantic HTML labels to improve accessibility. There will be three buttons, ordered as shown below.
| Previous image | Play sound | Next Image |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goes to the previous image in the collection | Plays the sounds generated by the image's color values | Goes to the next image in the collection |

In summary, the algorithm decides what instrument and what pitch it'll play based on the following.
| Pitch | Length and Volume | Stereo Position |
| ---- | ------------ | ------------ |
| Cooler colors, such as blues, sound lower. Warmer colors, such as reds and yellows, and brighter areas sound higher. The frequency obtained determines the instrument type to be played. | Large, uniform regions give long, louder notes. Small regions with a lot of detail give short, softer notes. | Regions on the left of the image play in your left ear, and regions on the right play in your right ear. |

Additionally, there's a gallery-styled menu to directly choose an image of the user's liking.

### Settings
The site offers various settings located at the bottom of the page, including the reading order ```preorder```, ```inorder```, or ```postorder```, Level of detail and whether the user wants to highlight the region being played.
